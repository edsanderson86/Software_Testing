Feature: Storefront customer journey
  Visitors can create an account, find products, build a basket and pay by card.
  Customers can return to a saved basket and see whether their payment created an order.

  Scenarios and example rows are the test conditions.
  Story, business requirement and test condition IDs provide traceability.
  Proposed refinements are tagged @proposed.
  Use test accounts, a test mailbox and the payment provider's sandbox.
